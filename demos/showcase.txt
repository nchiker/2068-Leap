sprdemo:
BORDER 3
PRINT "SPRITE DEMO"
INK 5
BLOCK 0,0 TO 7,7
SPRITE GRAB 0,0,0,1,1
CLS
INK 3
SPRITE GRAB 1,0,0,1,1
CLS
SPRITE SHOW 0,0,0
SPRITE SHOW 1,20,30
FOR I = 1 TO 15
SPRITE MOVE 0,I,I*2
SPRITE MOVE 1,20-I,30-I*2
IF HIT(0,1) <> 0 THEN GOSUB collide
PAUSE 5
NEXT I
SPRITE HIDE 0
SPRITE HIDE 1
PAUSE 50
CLS
GOTO snddemo

collide:
BORDER 6
SOUND 7,62
SOUND 8,15
SOUND 1,2
PAUSE 10
SOUND 8,0
BORDER 3
RETURN
